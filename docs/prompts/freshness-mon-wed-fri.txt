STAGE RUBRIC. not_started (0): no qualifying action found. proposed (5-20): announced, proposed rule, bill introduced, directed by an order, nothing operative. in_progress (25-60): formally underway, partly operative. implemented (65-90): operative in substance through executive or agency action, but reversible or short of the full recommendation. completed (100): done as specified and in effect. blocked (0-90, keeps earned progress): enjoined, vacated or stalled. diverged (0): materially different from or opposite to the recommendation. pending (0): not yet researched.
