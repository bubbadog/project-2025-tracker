STAGE RUBRIC. not_started (0): no qualifying action found. proposed (5-20): announced, proposed rule, bill introduced, directed by an order, nothing operative. in_progress (25-60): formally underway, partly operative. implemented (65-90): operative in substance through executive or agency action, but reversible or short of the full recommendation. completed (100): done as specified and in effect. blocked (0-90, keeps earned progress): enjoined, vacated or stalled. diverged (0): materially different from or opposite to the recommendation. pending (0): not yet researched. A rule that is final but not yet effective is in_progress.

CONFIDENCE (set only by the independent reviewer). high 80-100: document text checked and the stage-deciding primary source fetched and read. medium 55-79: text checked, key claims supported by a fetched reputable secondary source or partly by primary. low 21-54: rests on sources not opened, one secondary source, Wikipedia, or absence of evidence from a limited search (not_started is capped at 50). unverified 0-20.
